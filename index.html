05 About

Designer with an engineer's way of thinking.

I studied Electrical & Electronics Engineering before finding my way into UI/UX and digital product design. That path gave me a habit of breaking problems down before I try to solve them — a mindset that carries directly into how I approach interfaces.

I'm drawn to the parts of design that reward precision: information hierarchy, interaction states, the small details that decide whether a product feels considered or just assembled. I'm still early in this shift, and genuinely curious to keep learning from people further along than I am.
